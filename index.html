5. Fairness vs Efficiency Trade-off

Priority Scheduling prioritizes "urgency" based on assigned values, which is essential for critical tasks but can lead to starvation of low-priority processes. In contrast, SRTF maximizes system throughput and minimizes average waiting time by favoring shorter jobs, though it may delay longer tasks indefinitely if short jobs keep arriving.
